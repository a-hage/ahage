Privacy Policy for Quran & Duas – Haqibati

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Thank you for using Quran & Duas – Haqibati! This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle user data in our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app provides access to Islamic media and educational content (such as Quran recitations and audio resources). While the app accesses online resources to deliver media content, it does not track personal user identities or collect personal data.

2. Permissions and Data Handling

Our app only uses standard network permissions required to stream or download audio content for offline playback. We do not access your device's camera, contacts, precise location, or personal files.

3. Third-Party Services

The app relies on secure cloud infrastructure (such as Firebase Hosting / AWS) to serve media assets and host this privacy policy. These services process basic technical requests (like IP addresses for network routing) solely to deliver content to your device and do not build user profiles.

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect or request personal user data, your privacy is protected by design. All operational media delivery is handled through standard encrypted protocols (HTTPS).

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

In the event of any changes to our privacy practices or features, we will update this policy accordingly. Please check this policy regularly for updates.
